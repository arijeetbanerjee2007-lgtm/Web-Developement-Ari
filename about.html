In My Own Words

I am a curious and open-minded individual with an interest in exploring the intersection of finance, technology, creativity, and the world around me. I enjoy understanding how things work, exploring new ideas, and learning about subjects that challenge conventional thinking.

My interests span financial systems, emerging technologies, geopolitics, music, and storytelling. I value continuous learning, independent thinking, and developing a broader perspective through observation, research, and experience.

I aspire to build a versatile skill set, gain practical knowledge, and continue evolving personally and professionally.

Personal Philosophy
“Curiosity drives discovery. Knowledge builds perspective. Action creates progress.”

The Pursuit of Understanding

Rather than viewing disciplines in isolation, I find greatest fulfillment in connecting the dots between seemingly divergent domains. Why do markets behave the way they do? How do technological innovations rewrite human incentives? What makes an ancient myth or a modern musical score move our emotions across generations?

These questions form the bedrock of how I spend my time: questioning surface-level assertions, digging through source material, and distilling clarity from complexity.
